# Problem Statement

## 1. Project Title
AI-Powered Disaster Relief Task Allocation and Resource Management System

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Natural disasters such as floods, cyclones, earthquakes, and landslides create significant challenges in coordinating relief operations. Manual management of emergency requests, volunteer assignments, and resource distribution often results in delays, inefficient utilization of resources, and poor coordination. There is a need for an intelligent disaster relief management platform that enables faster response, effective communication, and AI-based optimization of relief operations.

---

## 3. Target Users
- Citizens
- Volunteers
- NGOs
- Government Authorities
- Disaster Management Teams

---

## 4. Core Features
- Secure User Registration and Login
- Disaster Reporting
- Emergency Request Management
- Volunteer Management
- Resource Management
- Relief Camp Management
- Task Assignment
- Real-time Notifications
- Dashboard and Reports
- AI-based Task Allocation and Resource Optimization

---

## 5. User Roles
### Admin
- Manage disasters
- Manage volunteers
- Allocate resources
- Monitor overall operations

### Volunteer
- View assigned tasks
- Update task status
- Manage relief activities

### Citizen
- Report disasters
- Raise emergency requests
- Track request status

---

## 6. Minimum Database Tables
1. Users
2. Disaster
3. Emergency_Requests
4. Volunteers
5. Resources
6. Task_Allocation
7. Relief_Camps

---

## 7. Success Criteria
- Citizens can successfully submit emergency requests.
- Volunteers receive and complete assigned tasks.
- Resources are allocated efficiently.
- AI recommends optimal task allocation based on priority.
- Admin can monitor relief operations through dashboards.

---

## 8. Out of Scope
- Drone-based rescue operations.
- Satellite image processing.
- Integration with Government emergency systems.
- Offline disaster communication networks.

---

## 9. Chosen Track
Python Full Stack (FastAPI + React)

---

## 10. AI Specialization
AI Task Allocation and Resource Optimization Model for Disaster Relief
